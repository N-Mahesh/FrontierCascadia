What am I doing?

You'd be the person at your school who makes sure students who would love this event actually hear about it. Concretely:

  • Get Frontier Cascadia into your school's announcements and newsletter.
  • Share it with your coding, robotics, entrepreneurship, AI, and STEM clubs.
  • Ask a teacher or counselor to circulate the registration link.
  • Aim for at least five completed student registrations through your personal referral link.

What you get

  • The official title Frontier Cascadia School Ambassador.
  • Verified volunteer and leadership hours.
  • A certificate and recognition on this website.
  • Access to the ambassador community and organizer meetings.
